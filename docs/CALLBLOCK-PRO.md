# CallBlock Pro
A simple, private list of telephone numbers you choose to block on your iPhone.

- Add complete international numbers with their country code.
- Remove numbers individually and refresh the iOS Call Directory list.
- Open the iPhone call-blocking settings directly from the app.
- Read guidance for country prefixes and WhatsApp privacy settings.
- Keep the number list on your device, with no developer account required.

iOS requires you to enable the Call Directory extension in Phone settings. Complete numbers are supported; country-prefix wildcards and WhatsApp call blocking are not supported. Manual number management is available without a subscription.
