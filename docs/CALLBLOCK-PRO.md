# CallBlock Mercury Pro
A simple, private list of telephone numbers you choose to block on your iPhone.

- Add complete international numbers with their country code.
- Remove numbers individually and refresh the iOS Call Directory list.
- Open the iPhone call-blocking settings directly from the app.
- Read guidance for country prefixes and WhatsApp privacy settings.
- Keep the number list on your device, with no developer account required.

iOS requires you to enable the Call Directory extension in Phone settings. Complete numbers are supported; country-prefix wildcards and WhatsApp call blocking are not supported. Download and prepare your number list for free. Activating telephone blocking requires an active annual subscription. Eligible new subscribers receive a 3-day free trial, followed by $19.99/year in the US storefront; localized prices and final terms are confirmed by Apple. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled before renewal.
