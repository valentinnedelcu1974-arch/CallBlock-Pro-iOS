# CallBlock Pro — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 9 November 2026

CallBlock Pro is provided by Iulian Valentin Nedelcu. Contact: valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com.

## Information stored on your iPhone
The complete telephone numbers you enter are stored locally in the app's shared iOS App Group so its Call Directory extension can provide the blocking list to iOS. They are not uploaded to the developer or a developer-operated server. Remove a number using its trash button. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior.

## Information we do not collect
The app does not send your blocking list, contacts, call history, call audio, location, advertising identifiers or usage analytics to the developer. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not require an account.

## Apple services
iOS processes the Call Directory list on your device. Apple handles any App Store purchase or restore through StoreKit under Apple's privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Device backups, diagnostic sharing and optional TestFlight feedback are controlled by your Apple settings and Apple services.

## Support and optional feedback
If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. Do not include private telephone numbers, call records or payment information unless necessary for your request. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request and may be deleted on request, except where retention is required by law. TestFlight feedback you voluntarily submit is handled through Apple's beta testing service.

## Children and changes
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. This page will be updated if the app's data practices change. Contact valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com with privacy questions or requests.
