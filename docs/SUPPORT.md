# CallBlock Mercury Pro — Support
Manage a personal list of complete international telephone numbers for iOS Call Directory blocking.

## Contact
Email **valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com** for CallBlock Mercury Pro support. Include your iOS version, app version, the action you tried and the message shown. Please omit private numbers from screenshots.

## Enable protection
1. Open CallBlock Mercury Pro and tap **Enable protection in iPhone settings**.
2. In iPhone Settings → Apps → Phone → Call Blocking & Identification, enable **CallBlock Mercury Pro**.
3. Return to the app and tap **Refresh phone blocking**.

## Add and remove numbers
Enter a full number with its country calling code, such as +441234567890 or 00441234567890, and tap **Add number**. Spaces, brackets and hyphens are accepted. Use the trash button to remove an entry. The saved list is refreshed through the extension; if iOS reports an error, enable the extension and retry Refresh.

## Limits and troubleshooting
Only complete numbers are supported. A country prefix is not a wildcard. The app cannot block WhatsApp calls, access call history or display verified blocked-call counts. For WhatsApp, use WhatsApp → Settings → Privacy → Calls → Silence Unknown Callers or block a contact within WhatsApp.

The extension needs a physical iPhone and must be enabled by you. If refreshing fails, confirm the extension is enabled, restart the app and retry. Report the exact iOS error to support if it persists.

Downloading the app and preparing or editing a saved number list are free. Eligible new subscribers receive 3 days free, then the annual subscription renews at the localized price confirmed by Apple (US base price: $19.99/year). Cancel through your Apple Account before the trial ends to avoid the annual charge. Apple determines introductory-offer eligibility. Restore uses the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

## Mercury Workspace
Open **Mercury Workspace · Inspect, import, export**. Paste one full international number per line or select a UTF-8 plain text file up to 1 MB. Imports allow at most 10,000 nonempty lines. Duplicate entries are consolidated. Invalid lines must be corrected or removed before applying new numbers. After saving, return to Protection and refresh. The saved-rule inspector checks only your own list, not a spam database or incoming call. Export shares personal phone numbers to the destination you choose.
