# CallBlock Pro — Support
Manage a personal list of complete international telephone numbers for iOS Call Directory blocking.

## Contact
Email **valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com** for CallBlock Pro support. Include your iOS version, app version, the action you tried and the message shown. Please omit private numbers from screenshots.

## Enable protection
1. Open CallBlock Pro and tap **Enable protection in iPhone settings**.
2. In iPhone Settings → Apps → Phone → Call Blocking & Identification, enable **CallBlock Pro**.
3. Return to the app and tap **Refresh phone blocking**.

## Add and remove numbers
Enter a full number with its country calling code, such as +441234567890 or 00441234567890, and tap **Add number**. Spaces, brackets and hyphens are accepted. Use the trash button to remove an entry. The saved list is refreshed through the extension; if iOS reports an error, enable the extension and retry Refresh.

## Limits and troubleshooting
Only complete numbers are supported. A country prefix is not a wildcard. The app cannot block WhatsApp calls, access call history or display verified blocked-call counts. For WhatsApp, use WhatsApp → Settings → Privacy → Calls → Silence Unknown Callers or block a contact within WhatsApp.

The extension needs a physical iPhone and must be enabled by you. If refreshing fails, confirm the extension is enabled, restart the app and retry. Report the exact iOS error to support if it persists.

Manual number management does not require a subscription. If Apple does not offer a subscription, the app says so. Any available purchase uses Apple's confirmation sheet; restore uses the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
